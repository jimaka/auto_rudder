small enough ω∗ such that the averaging and singular perturbation results hold for all ω ∈ (0, ω∗ ), the Lie bracket averaging
approach relies on the existence of a large enough ω∗ such that
for all ω > ω∗ the averaging results hold, with the actual required
value of ω∗ depending on the specific system being considered.
