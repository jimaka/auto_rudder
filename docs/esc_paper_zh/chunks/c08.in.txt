13. Hybrid ES with dynamic inclusions
For some systems, introducing the added flexibility of hybrid
controllers which switch between various feedback laws can be
advantageous and for others it is required due to the structure
of the system dynamics. Robust hybrid controllers have been
developed for autonomous vehicles operating under unknown
or adversarial environments, simultaneously avoiding obstacles
and source seeking (Poveda, Benosman, Teel, & Sanfelice, 2021),
hybrid ES controllers have been developed for optimization with
acceleration and resets (Poveda & Li, 2021), and a hybrid ES
approach has been developed for global practical optimization on
smooth compact manifolds (Strizic, Poveda, & Teel, 2017).
A general framework has been developed to extend the application of ES to hybrid systems with combined continuous
and discrete-time dynamics (Poveda et al., 2018; Poveda & Teel,
2017a). In Poveda and Teel (2017a) controllers were developed
for set-valued hybrid dynamical systems (HDS) of the form
